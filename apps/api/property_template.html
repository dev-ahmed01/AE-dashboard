LIVING TOGETHER

Good living, shared respect.

A few useful expectations for student accommodation, kept short and easy to scan.

● Example guidelines
01

Quiet and considerate living

Keep noise respectful in rooms and shared spaces, especially during study and rest periods.

02

Guests and visitors

Check guest sign-in, overnight-stay and visitor access requirements with the accommodation team.

03

Shared kitchens and lounges

Clean up after use, respect shared storage and leave communal spaces ready for others.

04

Smoking and vaping

Follow the building’s designated smoking and vaping policy and any restrictions on indoor use.

05

Fire and building safety

Keep exits clear, follow evacuation instructions and report safety concerns promptly.

06

Care of your room

Report damage or maintenance needs early and follow the room condition and deposit guidance.
