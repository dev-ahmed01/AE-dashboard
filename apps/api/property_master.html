Frequently asked questions

What is included in the rent? +

The listing includes Wi-Fi, electricity, water, heating and contents insurance. Confirm details and any exclusions in your contract.

What is the difference between an en suite and a studio? +

An en suite has its own bathroom and a shared kitchen. A studio has a private bathroom and its own kitchenette.

Can I reserve a specific room? +

Request a specific room or floor plan, subject to the operator's confirmed availability. An Accom enquiry is not a reservation.

Is there a guarantor requirement? +

A guarantor may be required for instalment payments. Confirm the current requirement before paying a booking fee.

Are viewings available? +

Ask the property team for the latest viewing or virtual tour options.
